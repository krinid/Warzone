Create Castles and have armies enter them to bolster defense on a given territory. Special Units cannot enter castles. A territory can only have 1 castle.

Castles do not protect armies or Special Units on the territory it resides on. To protect armies, they need to enter the castle.

Castles have configurable base cost, additional cost increase per castle owned and maintenance cost per turn. A conversion ratio controls how armies scale to castle health. Default value of 2 means that when 1 army enters a castle, it adds 2 strength to the castle, which defines both castle's Health and Defense Power.

Castle orders are entered from the Commerce menu (click the "Build" button). In the "Castles" section, there are options for:
• Purchase a Castle: use to build a new castle
• Armies Enter/Exit Castle: use to have units enter or exit a castle
• Scuttle Castle: use to destroy the castle and have all units inside exit

Order execution timing:
• Army Entry - same time as Emergency Blockade cards are played (after Bombs, before Attack/Transfers)
• Army Exit - same time as Blockade cards are played (after Attack/Transfers)
• Castle Scuttles - same time as Blockade cards are played (after Attack/Transfers)
• Castle Purchases - same time as new card pieces are received (after Attack/Transfers, after Castle Scuttles)
