Plant a landmine on any territory owned by you, a teammate (if in a team game) or a neutral territory within a host configurable range from your territories which explodes when the territory is captured. Enemies cannot see the landmine. You will be provided a reminder of the landmine via an Annotation at the end of each turn while a landmine is still deployed. Landmines do not expire, they remain on the map until triggered.

Damage can be assigned independently for armies and Special Units as follows:
(A) % damage dealt (0% - 100%)
(B) fixed damage (any integer)

% damage is applied first, then fixed damage is applied

Example: if configured to 25% damage + 10 fixed damage, a target territory with 100 armies would be reduced to 65 (100*0.75-10) after the Bomb  hits

In addition, Landmines can be configured to destroy cities
