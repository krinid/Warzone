Plant a time bomb on any territory within a host configurable range from your territories which explodes after a set number of turns. Enemies can see the time bomb.

Damage can be assigned independently for armies and Special Units as follows:
(A) % damage dealt (0% - 100%)
(B) fixed damage (any integer)

% damage is applied first, then fixed damage is applied

Example: if configured to 25% damage + 10 fixed damage, a target territory with 100 armies would be reduced to 65 (100*0.75-10) when the Time Bomb explodes

In addition, Time Bombs can be configured to destroy cities
