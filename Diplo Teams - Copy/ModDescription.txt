Provides Diplo functionality that supports native War.App Teams. When you join a Team/Alliance with another player, you join an actual native War.App team with that player. You can transfer units between teammates, can airlift to teammate territories, cards are shared among the team, and if you win the game, points are shared among the team.

Diplo functionality provides 3 relationship states between players:
• TEAMMATE/IN ALLIANCE - you are allied with another player, you are on the same War.App team; must be AT PEACE with a player in order to invite them to form a team
• AT WAR - you can freely attack a player you are at war with and vice versa; must be AT PEACE with a player in order to declare war on them
• AT PEACE - you cannot attack a player you are at peace with; you can move from TEAMMATE/ALLIANCE or AT WAR state to AT PEACE
• All state changes require 1 turn to execute
